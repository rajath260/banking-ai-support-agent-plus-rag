# Digital Payments FAQ (Fictional Demo Policy)

This document contains fictional policy examples for learning and testing. It is not an actual bank policy.

## What should I do if a UPI payment is pending?

If a UPI payment is pending, the customer should first check the transaction status in the official banking or UPI application. The customer should avoid repeating the payment until the status is clear, to reduce the risk of duplicate payment.

## What details are useful when reporting a failed payment?

The customer should keep the transaction reference number, transaction date and time, amount, and the status shown in the application. They should report the issue through the bank's official support channel.
