# Account Security FAQ (Fictional Demo Policy)

This document contains fictional policy examples for learning and testing. It is not an actual bank policy.

## Should I share my OTP or PIN with support staff?

No. A customer should never share an OTP, PIN, password, or full card security details with anyone, including someone claiming to be a bank employee.

## What should I do if I suspect unauthorized account access?

The customer should contact the bank immediately through an official channel, secure or block affected access where available, and review recent account activity. Any suspicious transaction should be reported promptly.
